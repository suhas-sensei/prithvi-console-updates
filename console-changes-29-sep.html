Changes how you work

Follow up stays until you remove it

What's different

  • Before, a customer's reply took the chat out of Follow up by itself. Now it stays in Follow up.
  • When the customer writes back, the chat shows Follow up · replied with an unread dot, and moves to the top of Follow up.
  • The Follow up count turns red while any customer in it has replied.

What to do

  • Check Follow up for "replied" chats and answer them first.
  • When the chat is active again, take it out yourself: remove from follow-up at the top of the chat, on the Tickets card, or right-click the chat in the list.
  • Resolving or handing off the chat also takes it out.
Changes how you work

Assign moved to the reply box

What's different

  • Assign is now in the row under the chat: Reply · Note · Template │ Assign.
  • It's no longer in the top bar of the chat. It works exactly as before.

Good to know

  • The list only shows teammates who are on shift and have room. If everyone is full, only a few names (or none) appear.
  • If nobody has room, use Send back to queue: the next agent who frees a seat gets it.
  • Handing off your own chat needs a short note on why (at least 20 characters). The teammate reads it.
New

De-escalate a red-alert chat with a note

What it's for

  • Your most-pushed chat is pinned to the top of My Tickets with a red alert. Once you've dealt with it, you can take the alert off.
  • The chat stays yours and stays open. Only the red alert comes off, and your next most-pushed chat moves to the top.

How

  • Open the red-alert chat and choose Note in the reply box.
  • Write what you did, tick De-escalate next to Add note, then add the note.
  • The tick only appears on red-alert chats you own. Leads can do it on any chat.
New

On call

What it does

  • Tap On call before you phone a customer. It's with your breaks, on the shift card and on the Breaks page.
  • While you're on a call, no new chats come to you, and your team sees "on call".
  • A timer at the top right counts up on every screen, with a red hang-up button.

Good to know

  • Calls don't use your break allowance. They count as work.
  • There's no time limit, so remember to Hang up when the call ends, or you won't get new chats.
  • If you resolve the chat over the phone, tick "Resolved over a phone call" and write what was said.
Fixed

Old notes no longer come back in the next chat

What was wrong

  • After resolving one chat, the next chat's Resolve sometimes showed the last chat's call summary.
  • A note you'd started but not sent could follow you into the next chat.

Now

  • Every chat starts with an empty note box and a clean Resolve window, with "Resolved over a phone call" unticked.
  • The De-escalate tick resets too, so it can't carry over to the wrong chat.
New

Dark and navy modes, and new ticks

Modes

  • Top right, just left of Live: three icons for Light, Dark and Navy.
  • Your choice is saved on your computer. Light stays the default.

For the escalation console

New ticket and search, moved

What's different

  • The "+ New ticket" button above each page is gone.
  • To raise an escalation case, use New ticket, now at the top of the Escalation group in the menu, above Overview. It works from any screen.

Search

  • The find box sits at the top centre of every escalation screen, including open cases and customer pages.
  • Type an order id, phone, email or name. It picks the right field for you.
Questions or something not working as described? Tell your team lead. Behind the scenes today there were also monitoring and error-reporting improvements; they don't change anything on your screen.
